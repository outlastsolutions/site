About Outlast Solutions

Outlast Solutions LLC is a technology systems and services company based in New York City. It designs and builds integrated technology systems — security, integration, consultation and office support — and provides on-site services in the surrounding areas. Outlast Solutions also runs three businesses of its own: Tribeca Notary, Low Volt NYC and Smart House DIY.
